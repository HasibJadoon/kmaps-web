The root بين pictures a gap opening between two things — and in that very parting, what was hidden steps into view. So بَيْن is the dividing middle, بَيان the making‑plain, and المُبين what is clear in itself and clarifies all else.

بَيانunveiling & exposition
بَيِّنةa clear proof
مُبينclear in itself, clarifying others
