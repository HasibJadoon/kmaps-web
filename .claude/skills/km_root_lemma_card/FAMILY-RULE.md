# What FAMILY means

**FAMILY = the ṣīgha family the lemma's own `Chain` declares** — the conjugational family of
ONE verb form: the verb with its prepositions, its passive, its ism fāʿil, its ism mafʿūl, its
maṣdar. **NOT the other forms of the root.** A form that declares its own chain is OUTSIDE and
has its own card.

## مُّبَٰرَكَة — Chain declares بارَكَ (فاعَلَ)

| | |
|---|---|
| Headword arc | مُبارَكة ×4 |
| Family | بارَكَ في ×5 · على ×1 · حولَ ×1 · بُورِكَ ×1 · مُبارِك · مُبارَك ×8 = **16** |
| card_total | 4 + 16 = **20** |
| Outside | تَبارَكَ (تَفاعَلَ) ×9 · بَرَكات (jāmid) ×3 = **12** |
| root_total | 20 + 12 = **32** ✓ |

تَبارَكَ is form VI. It declares its own chain, so it is outside — even though it is the same
root and the more famous word.

## كِتَٰب — Chain declares كَتَبَ (فَعَلَ)

| | |
|---|---|
| Headword arc | كِتَٰب ×260 |
| Family | كَتَبَ ×49 · كُتِبَ · كاتِب ×6 · مَكْتوب ×1 · كِتابِى ×1 = **57** |
| card_total | 260 + 57 = **317** |
| Outside | كاتَبَ (فاعَلَ) ×1 · اكْتَتَبَ (افْتَعَلَ) ×1 = **2** |
| root_total | 317 + 2 = **319** ✓ |

## The failure this file exists to prevent

A first attempt at كِتَٰب set the family to a **maṣdar set** — كَتْب، كِتابة، كِتْبة — reasoning
that كِتاب is a maṣdar so its family is the other maṣādir. Wrong on both counts: those are
alternative maṣādir of the *same verb*, not a ṣīgha family, and **all three have zero Qurʾānic
occurrences**, so `family_sum` came out 0 and the reconciliation failed silently.

**Read the Chain. Take exactly what it names. Nothing else.**

## When family_sum is genuinely 0

Record `0` with a note saying why. Do not omit the field. Do not reach outside the family to
find something to count.
